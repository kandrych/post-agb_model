AFTER THE GIANT — POST-AGB BINARY EXPLORER

OPEN
Open post-agb-explorer.html in a modern browser. No installation, server, internet
connection or external libraries are needed. Refresh an already-open tab after updates.

EXPLORE
Drag or use arrow keys to rotate; scroll or use + / - to zoom.
Auto-rotate changes camera azimuth; Animate flow shows schematic orbital motion,
inward gas feeding and companion outflows. View presets include a binary close-up.
Cutaway removes a viewing wedge, not physical material.

DUST AND SCATTERING
Show smooth grain layers displays overlapping, axisymmetric population envelopes:
small grains in the upper disk layers, intermediate grains at intermediate heights,
and larger grains concentrated toward the mid-plane and optionally toward the inner rim.
Settling and inner concentration controls change these representative distributions.
Sparse grain examples is optional and initially off. These symbols are exaggerated
samples, not individual resolved grains or a density map; unmarked regions contain dust.
The polarimetric highlight is the small-grain-bearing disk surface itself. A few surface
markers sit exactly on that layer. There is no detached scattering shell above the dust.
Hiding the small-grain population also hides its scattering highlight. Display switches
hide representations; they do not physically remove dust from the SED opacity model.
Scattering requires dust and illumination. The adopted surface is an approximate
optical-depth layer, not a solved radiative-transfer surface. Approximate surface illumination uses central-ray attenuation; angular shading uses
a prescribed intensity phase function times a polarization factor. These are not
grain-specific scattering calculations or a solved optical-depth surface. There are no imposed dust spirals or clumps.

GEOMETRY AND GAS
Edit representative geometry changes dust cavity size, rim puffing, surface height,
outer flaring and jet angle. Flaring beta = 1 means constant H/r; larger values open the
outer disk while holding the local dust-rim height fixed. Puffing raises the rim locally.
Inner dust-poor gas enables a reservoir interior to the dust rim and schematic feeding
of both the primary and the companion disk. The two branches can be hidden separately.
Inspect gas feeding selects a close-up; Animate flow moves particles inward.
Stream paths and rates are illustrative, not hydrodynamic trajectories. Particle counts
encode neither accretion fractions nor jet ejection efficiency. The companion is a faint
main-sequence star, with a muted non-glowing marker, not a specified brown dwarf.

TWO-COMPONENT LIVE SED
Gold: attenuated direct light from the post-AGB primary.
Orange: continuous-temperature thermal disk emission.
The white curve is the sum of these two components, not a third physical component.
There is no blue scattered-light curve, no
scattering term in the sum, and no companion component. This is a deliberate simplified
SED, not a claim that scattered light is negligible in every system or orientation.
The primary is an illustrative 6500 K blackbody. Thermal disk emission integrates
120 contiguous area-weighted blackbody annuli, with T = 1500 K (r/Rin)^(-0.65), to 400 Rin.
Spectral radius ratios map logarithmically onto the displayed dust interval; the scene
remains a compressed schematic without a uniform AU scale. Relative stellar heating,
cavity size and attenuated surface illumination modify the prescribed temperatures.
A diffuse-heating floor is assumed, not solved by radiative equilibrium.
A shared small-/large-grain Gaussian density and wavelength-dependent opacities
attenuate the primary. Settling preserves each population column mass. Inner
concentration modifies the large-grain radial density. The same vertically integrated
columns and opacity laws set approximate thermal emissivity.
The thermal disk uses a prescribed inclination-dependent projected-area factor.
Both curves share the same normalization: the peak of their SUM is one at each angle.
The log-flux axis displays faint tails; values below 1e-5 lie outside the plotted range.
The unobscured SED has a stellar peak and one broad infrared excess. Strong obscuration
may suppress the stellar peak; two visible maxima are not forced at every orientation.
Sweep inclination demonstrates face-on to edge-on viewing, not physical precession.
Cutaway, zoom and display visibility do not change the obscuring dust. Display switches only hide representations. Physical settling/concentration and
geometry controls now affect obscuration and approximate illumination; relative
stellar heating and cavity size also change the prescribed temperature profile.
There is no self-consistent energy balance or radiative-transfer solution.

SAVE & EDIT
Save image exports the scene as PNG with a scientific scale note.
Record 10 s captures the canvas only, not the SED or panels; format depends on browser.
Save settings exports JSON; Load settings restores it.
Save HTML exports a self-contained copy with current settings embedded.
Edit the HTML directly in a text editor; no build step is required.
MODEL contains display geometry; TECHNIQUES contains labels and descriptions;
SED_MODEL contains the illustrative spectral parameters; defaults contains initial controls.
Saved HTML copies use the saved-state JSON block to override defaults. Set it to null
to use edited defaults. All spatial scales and velocities are schematic; no AU scale applies.

SCIENTIFIC BASIS
See Science & references for linked papers. Post-AGB sources include Andrych et al.
(2023, 2024), Corporaal et al. (2023), Bollen et al. (2020, 2022), Verhamme et al. (2024),
De Prins et al. (2024), Kluska et al. (2022), Mohorian et al. (2025), and others.
Birnstiel (2024), Dust Growth and Evolution in Protoplanetary Disks, informs the visual
separation of small-grain scattering layers and settled large grains. Young-star disks
serve as a physical visualization analogue, not an identical evolutionary model.

GRAIN EXTENT & INDEPENDENT MARKERS
Large grains extend to MODEL.outer, the same outer boundary used by the millimetre
continuum layer. Inner concentration changes spacing, not that boundary.
Show grain markers works with smooth layers disabled. Grains only hides the context
disk, tracer overlays, smooth population envelopes, inner gas and jets, leaving the
stars and representative grain markers. The individual grain-size switches still apply.

NON-RT UPGRADE
Default: one surface tracer on a translucent neutral context disk. Opaque disk mode
has been removed. Continuous annular bands replace radial mesh wedges to reduce
rendering seams. Transparency is a display aid, not physical optical depth.
Depth ordering is approximate; it is not a full volume renderer.
Binary inset is independently enlarged, shares orbital phase and camera orientation,
and is an orientation diagram, not an apparent-brightness image.
Scattering-angle shading can be switched off. It changes brightness, never dust density.
Relative stellar heating is in Edit representative geometry.
Central illumination uses a barycentric source approximation; primary obscuration
uses its actual displayed orbital position. This distinction is deliberate and approximate.
No exact RT, hydrostatic equilibrium, grain-specific opacities, thermal energy balance,
resolved instrument response or target-specific fitting is claimed.
New code checks cover column conservation under settling, temperature response to
heating, rim attenuation, spectral-cache updates, opacity modes and saved settings.

VISIBILITY CONTROLS
Show/Hide binary detail beside the camera presets toggles the inset; the sidebar
Binary detail inset checkbox controls the same saved setting.
Both star markers are fully opaque and drawn over the disk at every viewing angle.
These are orientation annotations, not apparent-brightness predictions. The primary
remains larger/brighter than the companion; SED attenuation remains active.
The polarimetric overlay is display-enhanced for readability. Relative shadow/angle
shading remains, but displayed intensity is not calibrated polarized flux.

PRESENTATION VIEWS
Whole disk: complete, uncut neutral disk without observational overlays. This is
a physical schematic, not an observed image or true-colour reconstruction.
Probe layers: same physical geometry, cutaway enabled, with technique highlights.
Use Focus beside a technique to isolate its approximate sensitivity region.
Accretion: dedicated close-up with muted tapered supply ribbons and one inward
arrow per branch. Animate flow adds a short moving highlight; dense stream
particles have been removed. These supply routes are illustrative, not recovered
trajectories, and do not imply a measured division of the accretion rate.
Companion accretion disk: independently show/hide its mesh and label in the main
view and the inset. Jets and supply ribbons retain their own display switches.
The Whole disk and Probe layers presets change presentation settings, not
physical cavity size, disk height, flaring, settling or spectral parameters.

WHOLE-DISK MATERIAL
Whole disk now uses a warm inner rim grading smoothly to muted brown-grey outer
dust, a darker underside and a more solid silhouette. Whole-disk opacity adjusts
this view independently of technique overlays and physical SED attenuation.
Colours remain illustrative; this is not a calibrated visible-light image.

COLOUR SEPARATION
The whole disk uses muted copper-red and reddish-brown shading; the primary marker
is warm white in both the main scene and binary inset. Disk colours are illustrative
and do not encode a measured temperature or a true-colour photograph.

EDGE-ON BODY
The whole-disk view now includes nested, vertically tapered volume shells rather
than only upper/lower surfaces. Their accumulated opacity keeps the edge-on disk
substantial, while radial and vertical taper soften its boundary. This is a display
approximation, not a new RT solver, and does not change the SED.
The central cavity remains open; star markers are deliberately drawn in front.

CONNECTED SURFACE
The whole-disk body now retains opacity up to its boundary, shares the surface
colour prescription and overlaps the surface slightly to avoid rendering cracks.
The outer radial fade is retained. This display change leaves the SED unchanged.

STELLAR VISIBILITY BY VIEW
Whole disk: both stars are attenuated by their individual dust columns toward
the camera and can be hidden by the edge-on disk. They are not foreground markers.
Probe layers: both stars remain visible as explanatory foreground markers.
The optional binary inset remains an unattenuated orientation diagram.
